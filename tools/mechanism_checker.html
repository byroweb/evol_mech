    Mechanism

    Servo: L x W along the body, H along the shaft, shaft offset from one end. Thigh servo drives the thigh directly through its horn. Shin servo horn → coupler → pin on a tab on the shin tube (crank angle 15° = the pin sits ~11 mm aft of the shin axis so the coupler never rubs the tube). Default: the two servos flank the leg plane, both flat and at the same height; thigh servo inboard, horn at the front end of its block (body aft), hub out to the thigh tube; shin servo outboard, axis 45 mm aft, horn at the back end of its block (body forward), horn and coupler in the thigh plane, coupler pinned to a tab on the shin tube. The shin servo sits far enough aft that its face (6 mm off the plane) never meets the thigh tube (8 mm radius); 40 mm horn and crank (longer horns widen the reachable band). With the axis coaxial, crank angle 0 and equal horn / crank it becomes the LEO parallelogram.
